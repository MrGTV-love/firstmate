Valid literal/extglob case-pattern forms inside unquoted heredoc command substitution (head). Each must be selected on an unrelated leaf edit (parent-shell unknown source):
--- bin/lc1.sh:
cat <<EOF
$(case x in (esac|done) . "$DYN";; esac)
EOF
selected_on_unrelated_edit=1
--- bin/lc2.sh:
cat <<EOF
$(case x in done|in) . "$DYN";; esac)
EOF
selected_on_unrelated_edit=1
--- bin/lc3.sh:
cat <<EOF
$(case x in a) echo;; (esac) . "$DYN";; esac)
EOF
selected_on_unrelated_edit=1
--- bin/lc4.sh:
cat <<EOF
$(case $(. "$DYN") in esac) :;; esac)
EOF
selected_on_unrelated_edit=1

Note: fixture heredoc-literal-case-unknown.sh ('case x in esac|done)') is invalid bash: bash reports 'syntax error near unexpected token done' and never runs the source; ShellCheck reports SC1010, so it is never cached as a success either.

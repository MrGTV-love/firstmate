## note added after the run (read before trusting the timings above)
Python start-up on this host takes 1.5-4 s (load5 ~60), which skews the timings in this file.
- A: "--wait-file ms: 0" and 8.56 s elapsed mean the waiter reached the pool after the filler had ended. See manual-pass-wait-and-kill-timed.txt (A2: waited 10376 ms, started 1.21 s after the filler ended).
- B: the "+3 s still queued" check may have run before the waiter reached the queue. See slot-wait-base-vs-head.txt, which uses in-command time stamps (base admits the waiter while the holder runs; head waits until the holder ends).
- C: superseded by C2 in manual-pass-wait-and-kill-timed.txt (waiter started 0.67 s after SIGKILL of the holder).
- E: the count of 4 includes the bash -c wrapper. The proof is the three ~1.0 s elapsed progress lines from three 2 s sleeps running together.

## Note on top=0 under sandbox-exec
/bin/ps is setuid root (-rwsr-xr-x). sandbox-exec refuses to exec setuid binaries:
  sandbox-exec: execvp() of '/bin/ps' failed: Operation not permitted
So top=0 in every sandboxed run above is a sandbox artifact. It shows the guard degrades a failed listing to [] without error.
The unsandboxed runs (s1, s3) show the real listing.
